# Coaching playbook

Use this reference to choose high-value questions and interventions. Do not run every section as a questionnaire.

## Start well

Open with the current value, not a long welcome:

- “先给你一个初步判断：这更像是环境问题，不一定是赛道问题。要验证它，我还需要两个信息。”
- “你现在真正要决定的不是‘要不要辞职’，而是‘是否有足够证据把不满归因于这个职业’。”
- “我可以先给倾向，再用几个问题校准；也可以陪你系统梳理。你更想要哪种？”

When enough context exists, skip permission-seeking and help immediately.

## Career direction

Useful questions:

- Which recent tasks created energy after completion, not only during novelty?
- What outcomes do other people repeatedly trust the user to produce?
- Which constraints are truly non-negotiable for the next two years?
- What is the user unwilling to give up in exchange for growth?
- Which option has been romanticized without contact with its daily work?

Interventions:

- Compare energizing tasks rather than job titles.
- Look for evidence in behavior, results, and repeated demand.
- Separate a need for recovery from a need for reinvention.

## Transition or resignation

Useful questions:

- Is the user moving toward a specific opportunity or only away from pain?
- Has the same problem appeared across multiple environments?
- What financial and emotional runway exists?
- What could be tested before resigning?

Interventions:

- Build a bridge plan: evidence, capability, relationships, applications, and runway.
- Name avoidable risk directly.
- Consider internal transfer, role redesign, leave, or workload change as real options, not failures of courage.

## Promotion and leadership

Useful questions:

- What does the next level own that the current level does not?
- Which next-level outcomes are already demonstrated with evidence?
- Who decides, who influences, and who can sponsor the case?
- What feedback has been requested explicitly from the manager?

Interventions:

- Convert aspiration into an evidence map and stakeholder plan.
- Rehearse the promotion conversation with a specific ask and date.
- Distinguish a skill gap from an opportunity, visibility, or sponsorship gap.

## Job search and Offer choice

Useful questions:

- What are the top three criteria, and which one is truly non-negotiable?
- What does the manager expect in the first six months?
- Which information comes from evidence versus recruitment sales language?
- Which choice improves future options even if the role lasts only two years?

Interventions:

- Run a structured diligence plan before scoring.
- Compare downside cases, not only best cases.
- Negotiate only after clarifying priorities and alternatives.

## Burnout and unsustainable work

Do not diagnose. Explore practical contributors such as workload, control, reward, community, fairness, values conflict, recovery, and non-work stress.

Useful questions:

- What has changed in demands, control, recovery, or meaning?
- Which part is acute and which has persisted across roles?
- What can be reduced, delayed, delegated, or renegotiated this week?
- Is the user safe and able to function, or is qualified health support needed?

Interventions:

- Stabilize load and recovery before demanding a major identity decision.
- Design a manager conversation with concrete observations and requests.
- Encourage qualified support for persistent, severe, or worsening symptoms.

## Conflict and difficult conversations

Separate:

- observed behavior;
- the meaning assigned to it;
- impact on work or trust;
- the request or boundary;
- the other person's likely interests and constraints.

Use a concise script:

```text
When [specific observation] happened, the impact was [concrete impact].
I may be missing context. How do you see it?
Going forward, I need/request [specific behavior or decision].
```

Do not encourage confrontation where retaliation or safety risk is material; plan documentation and appropriate support.

## Accountability and follow-up

Close a substantive session with:

- decision or working hypothesis;
- smallest next action;
- deadline;
- evidence of completion or learning;
- likely obstacle and response;
- follow-up date when the user wants accountability.

On follow-up, ask what happened before explaining what should have happened. Update the plan from evidence, not shame.

## Avoid these failure modes

- Ten reflective questions with no point of view.
- A framework dump that never touches the user's actual choice.
- Generic encouragement such as “相信自己” or “走出舒适区.”
- Treating salary, prestige, or passion as universally dominant.
- Assuming every career problem requires changing careers.
- Presenting a numerical score as more certain than the underlying evidence.
- Calling ordinary discomfort a “limiting belief” without checking real constraints.
